Hydrogen peroxide vs. chlorhexidine rinse after gum surgery

A randomized controlled trial in BMC Oral Health compared a hydrogen-peroxide mouthwash with chlorhexidine for healing after periodontal flap surgery. Because chlorhexidine is the long-standing standard but is poorly tolerated by some patients, a credible alternative would be clinically useful.

Psychology

Adults — recent, DSM-5-related

Neuroticism predicts who stays distressed after a crisis

A USC-led study using a ~14,000-adult U.S. panel (tracked 2019 to early 2024) found the most neurotic quarter of people saw distress rise about 1.1 points more on a brief depression/anxiety scale during the pandemic, and the gap persisted into 2024. The other four "Big Five" traits showed much weaker links. It bears on DSM-5 depressive/anxiety disorders because the outcome is a standard distress measure, suggesting reactivity to uncertainty can flag vulnerability to prolonged distress.

In late January 2026 the American Psychiatric Association published five papers in the American Journal of Psychiatry describing the next DSM revision as an online, continuously updatable resource rather than a single print volume, so criteria can track new evidence faster. No firm timeline is set, and it is undecided whether it will be called DSM-6.

Children — the ~6-year-old brain (for Andrei, b. 2020)

Shift into logical ("concrete operational") thinking

Around age 6 children move out of preschool "magical thinking" and begin reasoning about cause and effect, grasping time (and starting to tell it), understanding quantities, and following rules. Developmental research stresses this is gradual and task-specific, so expect uneven progress across skills. Watch this year: following multi-step instructions, early reading fluency and number sense, sustained attention.

Verified Columbia Doctors Kaiser Permanente

Executive function and consolidated theory of mind

By ~6, children reliably pass standard false-belief ("Sally-Anne") tasks, and the early school years bring rapid growth in working memory, inhibitory control and cognitive flexibility — closely linked to social understanding. Watch this year: impulse control, ability to wait and take turns, flexible problem-solving.

Verified / Partial Frontiers in Psychology

Social-emotional growth, friendships and body changes

Six-year-olds form deeper friendships and a stronger sense of fairness but still need adult help regulating big feelings, and some earlier fears can persist. Physically, first permanent molars arrive and baby teeth start to fall out, with motor gains (two-wheel bike, catching, scissors, tying shoes). Watch this year: emotional regulation, peer relationships and cooperative play; raise any developmental concern early.

Educating a ~6-year-old (evidence-based)

Authoritative parenting: warmth plus clear structure

Studies converge that authoritative parenting — high warmth and responsiveness combined with firm, consistent expectations — predicts better emotion management, self-control and peer interaction, while authoritarian and permissive styles predict worse regulation. Two independent 2023 studies (Chinese and Indonesian samples, ages 3–6) found this association, though the evidence is correlational.

Verified (correlational) Frontiers in Psychology

Keep reading aloud; favor print over screens

The AAP's updated literacy policy recommends reading with children from birth and continuing at least through kindergarten, framing shared reading as relationship-building that stimulates brain circuitry in a critical window; touchscreens tend to be passive and lack the same interactive benefit. An MRI study of 3–5-year-olds linked a rich home reading environment to stronger brain activation during story listening. For a 6-year-old entering independent reading, adult read-aloud remains valuable.

Verified / Partial Neuroscience News PBS NewsHour

Use tested programs for behavior; coach emotion regulation

For behavior and the parent-child relationship, programs with strong randomized-trial evidence include Triple P and The Incredible Years, which reduce behavior problems and improve the relationship; by contrast, branded "gentle parenting" has not been rigorously tested as a complete system. In practice for a 6-year-old: consistent routines, naming and coaching big feelings rather than only punishing them, and clear, predictable limits.

Medicine

FDA approves atezolizumab + chemo for stage III dMMR colon cancer

The FDA approved atezolizumab (Tecentriq) with fluoropyrimidine and oxaliplatin as adjuvant treatment for adults and children (2+) with stage III mismatch-repair-deficient (dMMR) colon cancer — the first immunotherapy-based adjuvant regimen in this setting. In the ATOMIC trial, adding atezolizumab cut the risk of recurrence or death by 50% (HR 0.50; P=.0001). A subcutaneous formulation was approved alongside it.

Verified2026-10-08 ASCO Post OncLive

FDA approves remibrutinib — first drug for symptomatic dermographism

Novartis's oral BTK inhibitor remibrutinib (Rhapsido) became the first FDA-approved therapy for adults with symptomatic dermographism, a form of chronic inducible urticaria, whose symptoms persist despite antihistamines. It is now the only prescription treatment cleared for both chronic spontaneous urticaria and symptomatic dermographism.

Verified2026-10-07 AJMC The Dermatologist

FDA approves once-monthly injectable olanzapine for schizophrenia

Teva received FDA approval for Weltruza, a once-monthly subcutaneous long-acting injectable olanzapine for adults with schizophrenia, supported by the Phase III SOLARIS study. It is Teva's second long-acting schizophrenia product after Uzedy, with a U.S. launch planned in the following weeks. The exact approval date could not be independently confirmed.
