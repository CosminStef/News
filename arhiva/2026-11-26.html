Courses & the English-language online CE market

Implantology remains the best-monetised English-language online CE category

Across global vendor academies the pattern is consistent: implant education is the anchor of paid English-language online dentistry courses sold internationally (Nobel Biocare reports 14,000+ professionals a year through its implant programs; Straumann/ITI, Dentsply Sirona and EAO run comparable English catalogues). Dental "AI receptionists" mature into standalone front-office products

🧠 4 · Psychology

Adults — finding of the day (DSM-5 lens)

GLP-1 drugs associated with lower alcohol use

A real-world survey found that about half of people who drink and take GLP-1 medications (the semaglutide/Ozempic class) reported drinking less, with the steepest drops among those at higher risk of unhealthy use. The authors are careful: this screened for "potentially unhealthy" drinking, not diagnosed DSM-5 Alcohol Use Disorder, and self-report cannot prove magnitude — but it converges with a BMJ Open cohort (~63% fewer alcohol-related hospitalisations) and a 2026 NIH randomised trial showing semaglutide cut drinks-per-day and cravings. Promising, not yet an approved AUD treatment.

18 Nov 2026Verified
Sources: Harvard Gazette · EurekAlert · NIH
Children — Andrei (born 2020, ~6 years old)

The logic years begin: cause-and-effect firms up, "magical thinking" fades

Around six, children start grasping genuine cause-and-effect, begin to understand time, and the preschooler's magical thinking recedes quickly. Most can state their age, work with the idea of "ten," and are actively learning to write — the brain is laying the logical scaffolding for early academics. This is the window to lean into "why" questions and simple reasoning games.

EvergreenVerified
Sources: Columbia Doctors · Alberta Health

Social-emotional skills are this year's biggest growth area

At six, the fastest development is social-emotional and thinking skills: cooperative play lengthens (20+ minutes with two or three peers), friendships and fairness start to matter, yet typical preschool fears (the dark, monsters, being alone) often linger. Over the coming year, watch for the ability to follow multi-step game rules, manage frustration, and separate comfortably at school — and treat lingering fears as normal rather than a problem.

📚 5 · Education

The next ~20 years vs. the classical school

OECD: generative AI helps only with deliberate pedagogy

The OECD's Digital Education Outlook 2026 finds that general chatbots can raise the quality of students' work while they have access, but the gain fades — and can reverse — on closed-book exams. Purpose-built educational AI, designed around learning goals, produces more durable gains and can noticeably lift less-experienced tutors. The 20-year signal: AI augmenting teaching, not replacing the classroom, with equity, infrastructure and academic-integrity policy as the hard part.

Jan 2026 reportVerified
Source: OECD

WEF: ~39% of core skills shift by 2030 — the case for skills over static knowledge

The World Economic Forum projects that around 39% of workers' core skills will change by 2030 and that 63% of employers see skill gaps as the top barrier to transformation. Analytical thinking stays the most-valued skill, with AI/big-data, cybersecurity and environmental literacy rising fastest — pointing to curricula weighted toward reasoning, AI literacy and continuous reskilling rather than fixed content mastery.

2025 reportVerified
Source: WEF Future of Jobs 2025
Educating a child of Andrei's age (~6)

Pair short explicit instruction with guided, goal-directed play

A Child Development review of 17 studies (ages 3–8) found that guided play — play with an adult-set learning goal, gently steered — can match direct instruction for some literacy and executive-function gains. The practical recipe at six: brief, explicit phonics/reading and number practice, combined with purposeful play (word games, story role-play, building and drawing that involve writing and counting). Play supplements structured literacy and math; it doesn't replace it. Daily shared reading feeds oral language and letter-level skills, and rule-based cooperative games build the self-regulation that underpins school readiness.

Sources: EdSource · New America

The FDA approved Regeneron's cemdisiran for adults with antibody-positive generalised myasthenia gravis — the first small-interfering-RNA (siRNA) medicine cleared for this autoimmune neuromuscular disease, and Regeneron's first neurology approval.

The FDA approved Beren Therapeutics' adrabetadex to reduce the risk of death in infantile-onset Niemann-Pick disease type C — the first therapy shown to improve survival in this rare, fatal neurodegenerative disorder of childhood.
