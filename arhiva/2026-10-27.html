Education

Education over the next ~20 years vs. the classical school

OECD's four scenarios for schooling to ~2040

The OECD's "Back to the Future of Education" lays out four scenarios: (1) Schooling extended — today's school intensifies; (2) Education outsourced — private and on-demand learning markets explode; (3) Schools as learning hubs — school redefined as a community hub; (4) Learn-as-you-go — formal schooling dissolves into continuous, technology-mediated learning. The OECD is explicit that these are thinking tools, not predictions, and the classical model survives as one scenario rather than a certainty.

How to educate a child of Andrei's age (~6)

Read aloud, teach reading explicitly, and talk about screens together

The AAP describes shared read-aloud time as one of the most effective ways to expose a child to rich language and build early literacy. For learning to read, the "science of reading" / structured-literacy consensus favors explicit, systematic phonics in sequence, grounded in the Simple View of Reading (word recognition × language comprehension); the primary peer-reviewed reference is the National Reading Panel (2000). Screen content — including educational apps — supports learning mainly when it is well-designed and an adult discusses it with the child (co-viewing), not passive watching. A reported 2026 AAP "5 C" screen-time framework (Child, Content, Calm, Crowding Out, Communication) circulated via secondary sources but could not be confirmed directly from the AAP.
