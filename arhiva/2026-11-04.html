Adults — recent, DSM-5-framed finding

No meaningful age gap in treatment outcomes for anxiety, OCD or PTSD — but older adults are treated less

A study in the Journal of Anxiety Disorders compared psychological-treatment outcomes between younger and older adults treated for DSM-5 anxiety disorders, OCD or PTSD in routine mental-health care. It found no meaningful difference in outcomes by age, yet older adults were less likely to be referred or treated — pointing to an access gap rather than an efficacy gap. The full effect sizes could not be opened to confirm.

Developmental guidance (reputable developmental-psychology / health-system sources), not breaking news. These are typical ranges, not fixed checkpoints.

Medicine — discoveries & firsts

This week's confirmed results cluster around TCT 2026 (Transcatheter Cardiovascular Therapeutics, San Diego, 31 Oct–3 Nov), several with simultaneous journal publication. No novel FDA drug approval was dated within the last 72h.

PROGRESS trial: early TAVR beats watchful waiting in moderate aortic stenosis

In a randomized trial of 744 patients over 65 with moderate aortic stenosis plus a risk feature, early transcatheter valve replacement cut the 2-year composite of death or heart-failure events to 16.7% versus 23.9% with surveillance, driven mainly by fewer heart-failure events. There was no clear mortality benefit at 2 years, and most of the surveillance arm eventually needed a valve anyway. It is the first randomized evidence for intervening before stenosis becomes severe.

Verifiedpresented 2 Nov 2026 (NEJM)
NEJM·MedicalXpress

P4 trial: CT-angiography guidance non-inferior to intravascular ultrasound for stenting

In 1,105 patients, planning coronary stent procedures with coronary CT angiography was non-inferior to the gold-standard intravascular ultrasound for 1-year target-vessel failure, while shortening the procedure (median 46 vs 63 minutes). It is the first large randomized head-to-head suggesting a non-invasive imaging strategy can match IVUS. The trial was sponsored by Heartflow.

Verifiedpresented 1 Nov 2026 (NEJM)
TCTMD·GlobeNewswire

TRANSFORM II: sirolimus-coated balloon vs drug-eluting stent in small vessels

A multicentre non-inferiority trial (1,820 patients) compared a sirolimus-coated balloon against a new-generation drug-eluting stent in small (2–3mm) coronary arteries, with the 1-year endpoint presented at TCT and acceptance for publication in The Lancet.
