Adults — science-backed finding of the day

Does the aging brain "trade memory for wisdom"?

A theoretical paper in Perspectives on Psychological Science argues cognitive aging is not simply decline: the brain may shift from retaining fine detail toward broader, semantic, big-picture understanding. It is a conceptual proposal, not new experimental data, and bears on where normal aging ends and DSM-5 neurocognitive disorder begins.

Children — the brain of a ~6-year-old (Andrei, b. 2020)

What is developing, and what to watch this coming year

Cognitive: thinking shifts toward cause-and-effect reasoning as preschool "magical thinking" fades; a sense of time emerges; reading and writing begin; the child can typically count to and grasp "10". Physical: ~2.5 inches and ~7 lbs of growth over the year, first permanent (6-year) molars erupting, baby teeth being lost. Social-emotional: parents stay the primary source of comfort, but friends and admired adults (teachers) grow in importance; pretend play is common and some preschool fears persist.

Watch for: children progress unevenly, so the overall pattern matters more than any single milestone. Keep an eye on reading/writing readiness, the ability to follow multi-step instructions, emotional regulation, and peer interaction — and raise concerns early (the CDC's free Milestone Tracker app helps; note 6 is not a formal CDC checkpoint, which sits at 5 then 8).

Education

The next 20 years vs. the classic school model

Forecasts converge on "scenarios, not a single path"

The most rigorous framing is the OECD's "Back to the Future of Education", with four scenarios to 2040 ranging from schooling extended and more diverse, to education outsourced to markets, to schools as community learning hubs, to traditional schooling dissolving into "learning everywhere". The World Economic Forum offers a similar four-scenario set, and IE University's "Education in 2050" leans toward self-guided, personalized learning and the disintermediation of traditional institutions. Treat all 20-year predictions as scenarios — past forecasts (e.g. 1990s mass-homeschooling calls) aged poorly.

VerifiedReference / analyses
OECD WEF IE University

How to educate a child Andrei's age (~6)

Guided play with clear goals, plus some explicit instruction

The best-supported approach at this age is adult-guided play with explicit learning goals. The Education Endowment Foundation's early-years toolkit finds play-based learning has a moderate positive effect (~+4 months progress), with guided play better evidenced than free play. Pair it with explicit instruction in early reading/phonics and numeracy, and prioritize implementation quality (good instructional design, trained adults). Note the evidence is largely extrapolated from 0–8 and 6–12 research, not studies of 6-year-olds alone.

Medicine

New discoveries and firsts

FDA accelerated approval: Ultomiris for IgA nephropathy

AstraZeneca's Alexion said the FDA granted accelerated approval for Ultomiris (ravulizumab) in adults with primary IgA nephropathy at risk of progression — the first C5 complement inhibitor for this kidney disease. The nod rests on a week-34 interim analysis showing a 43.4% placebo-adjusted reduction in proteinuria; continued approval may hinge on kidney-function (eGFR) data at week 106.

VerifiedOct 21, 2026
AstraZeneca Yahoo/Reuters

FDA approves Hibsago (bepirovirsen) for chronic hepatitis B

The FDA approved the first antisense oligonucleotide for chronic hepatitis B — GSK and Ionis's bepirovirsen — for use alongside existing antivirals in certain adults without cirrhosis. Phase III B-Well data supported a functional-cure rate of roughly 19% after about six months; a companion diagnostic was also cleared.

VerifiedOct 20, 2026
FDA AJMC

Brepocitinib hits endpoints in non-infectious uveitis (CLARITY-1 & -2)

Roivant and Priovant reported both pivotal Phase 3 trials of oral brepocitinib met their primary endpoint (time to treatment failure). Pooled across 371 patients, 62% achieved steroid-free quiescence at week 48 vs 20% on placebo, with no new safety signals; an FDA filing is planned for H1 2027.
