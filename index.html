Children — the brain of a ~6-year-old (for Andrei, b. 2020)

What is developing at six, and what to watch over the coming year

At six, the prefrontal cortex is developing fast — driving gains in attention, planning and impulse control — while synaptic pruning and myelination make thinking quicker. Reasoning turns concrete and logical, "magical thinking" fades, and grasp of cause-and-effect and of time firms up; many children begin reading independently and forming friendships around shared interests. Some separation anxiety and lingering fears (dark, monsters) remain normal. Watch over the next year (6→7): steady literacy/numeracy progress, growing emotional self-regulation, lengthening attention span, and stable peer friendships — all typical ranges, not hard requirements. Raise concerns with a pediatrician.
