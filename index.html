Children — the brain of a ~6-year-old (Andrei, b. 2020)

What is developing now, and what to watch this coming year

Developing now: at ~6 the brain consolidates cause-and-effect reasoning while preschool "magical thinking" fades; a concept of time emerges; sustained attention stretches to roughly 15 minutes; reading and writing come online, and vocabulary expands fast (sounds like "r"/"th" may still be imperfect). Socially, parents remain the anchor but the child begins meeting more emotional needs through friends and admired adults such as teachers. Physically, first permanent molars erupt and baby teeth start to be lost.

Watch over the next year: persistent difficulty with reading/letters (possible learning-difference signal), something quietly bothering him (e.g. bullying), lingering fears (monsters, large animals), and sleep/attention changes. These are screening flags for a pediatrician, not diagnoses.
